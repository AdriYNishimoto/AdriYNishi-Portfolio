# NISHIMOTO — logo v1

Criada com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens.

Arquivo final: `public/brand/nishimoto-logo-dark-v1.png`.
Versão com fundo escuro, nome claro e monograma índigo. Não é um arquivo transparente ou vetorial.

## Direção inicial

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: finished transparent-background logo for a personal developer portfolio website.
Create one refined, original, minimalist professional horizontal logo. The exact main wordmark is "NISHIMOTO" (N I S H I M O T O), perfectly spelled, uppercase, on a single line, and it must dominate the composition.
Design direction: sophisticated contemporary Swiss/Japanese graphic restraint. Custom geometric neo-grotesk letterforms, medium to semibold weight, exquisite optical kerning, clean easily readable forms. A subtle diagonal cut on the initial N gives the wordmark a distinctive signature; avoid over-stylizing the other letters. At the left, a small compact geometric N monogram drawn from two vertical stems and one precise diagonal connector, with a tiny intentional negative-space break, balanced and simple enough for a favicon. Monogram should be approximately 1.2 times capital height, separated from the wordmark by generous clear space. The name must be far more visually important than the icon.
Colors: wordmark in near-white #EDEDED, monogram in restrained periwinkle indigo #818CF8, designed for placement on the portfolio's charcoal #0A0A0B background. Background must actually be transparent, preserving alpha; do NOT paint a checkerboard or charcoal rectangle.
Composition: one horizontal lockup only, centered on a landscape canvas around 3:1, sensible clear space all around, logo fills most of the width. Flat crisp vector-like edges, careful alignment, high resolution. No slogan, no extra text, no Japanese characters, no borders, no circle badge, no gradients, no glow, no shadow, no textures, no mockup, no multiple options. Elegant and assured, not a gaming or aggressive esports logo. Deliver a usable isolated transparent logo.

## Prompt de refinamento do arquivo final

Edit the previous NISHIMOTO logo. Keep the exact spelling, horizontal layout and the overall geometric N monogram. Make this a professionally finished clean flat logo by removing ALL the visual defects: stray white speckles outside letters and inside counters, colored cyan fringes, scratches, thin black edge outlines, bevels and gradients. Wordmark should be solid near-white #EDEDED with perfect clean edges; N monogram solid periwinkle #818CF8, no shading. Slightly increase space between letters so adjacent letters do not touch. Keep exact NISHIMOTO spelling. This time use an entirely uniform opaque solid charcoal #0A0A0B background for a clean portfolio-ready presentation, no transparency. All empty areas including inside letters must be the identical charcoal background color. No extra elements or text, no multiple designs. Wide landscape image with centered logo and balanced clear space.
